Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

NFP Publisher is a personal publishing tool operated by its owner for the owner's own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It has no other users.

Data accessed

With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth / Instagram Login), the tool accesses only the owner's own channel data needed to publish and manage content: channel and video metadata, uploads, captions, thumbnails, playlists, and basic statistics. It uses the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private GitHub repository and are used only to run the owner's publishing jobs. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Video files and text are those the owner chose to publish.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions and in Instagram settings (Apps and websites).
